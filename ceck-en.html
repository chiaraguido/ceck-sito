From quote to handover

Twelve job-site problems. One app.

Quotes, planning, hours, money, site walks, measurements, materials, contracts. ceck keeps it all together, on your phone, while you work. And it tells you first what you usually find out too late.

Made by a site manager, after 43 years on building sites.

Sound familiar?

For each one, the real ceck screen.

01

“My price list is in Excel and I rewrite every quote from scratch.”

The quote starts from your price list.

Import your Excel as it is. Every item has its breakdown: labour and materials, adhesive, primer, tiles.

02

“I only find out at the end of the job that I lost money.”

You see it while you work, not after the job is closed.

Planned hours are installation hours only. Next to the work done there is the ring of hours used: if you use more hours than you have installed, it turns red.

03

“I make the schedule once and then never look at it again.”

See the future of the job. And the future is worth money.

The schedule comes from the quote, with real people: one plumber can’t do two jobs at once. Link tasks with two taps.

04

“I don’t know how much I make on what my subcontractors do.”

Every supplier product has its own markup.

The supplier has its own price list. On every item you see what you pay, what you sell it for and what you make. ceck works out the subcontractor’s contract.

05

“In the evening I copy the crew’s hours from paper sheets and messages.”

Thirty seconds for the whole crew.

Tick who was there, write the hours, done. Under each name, their trade.

75 m² flat renovation · Via Roma 12, Verona · 2nd floor

06

“Snags get lost between photos and messages.”

Every defect is a point on the plan.

A photo, the exact spot, who it’s for. Red to do, yellow in progress, green fixed.

07

“Site walks: photos everywhere, notes in a notebook, the report written at night.”

Walk the site. The report is already done.

Photo, a finger mark on what’s wrong, spot and viewing angle on the plan. Every note goes to someone.

08

“I need to jot down two measurements on site and don’t know where.”

One finger is enough.

Take a photo of the opening, press and drag from end to end and type the measurement. It stays on the photo.

09

“I order materials by phone and then nobody remembers what.”

The supplier order in one minute.

Type the quantities, ceck sends the PDF to the supplier and keeps a copy for you.

10

“A worker’s contract runs out and nobody notices.”

Thirty days before, a yellow dot.

A blinking dot lights up on the Priorities tile: inside you find whose contract is ending.

⚠ PrioritiesKarim Benali: contract ends in 25 days
11

“My crew speaks five languages.”

Everyone reads in their own language.

English, Italian, Romanian, French, Spanish, German, Polish. Under every text, the second language.

12

“The client calls every day to ask when I’ll finish.”

The client sees the schedule, not your numbers.

Invite them for free: they see how far along you are and when you’ll finish. No hours, no prices. Fewer calls.

Why it works

It’s not office software. It’s made for people on site.

Big buttons, little text, works even with your gloves just off. If you don’t know how, ask the help: it answers in your language.
