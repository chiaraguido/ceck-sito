By signing up, the company declares it acts for its business. Signing up means accepting these terms.

2 · Sign-up and accounts

The person who signs up the company creates its “ceck” and becomes responsible for the subscription. Each person has their own account: passwords and access are not shared. The information given must be true and up to date.

3 · Roles and invitations

Each person joins with a role (management, project manager, site manager, office, store, subcontractor owner, foreman, worker, client). The role decides what they see and can do. Invitees join only if they accept. Important actions are logged with name, date and time.

4 · Free trial

Every new ceck has a 21-day free trial, no card required. If you find a defect in ceck during the trial, report it to app@ceck.me: the 21 days start again from zero. At the end of the trial, without a subscription, access is suspended; data is kept for 3 months, then deleted, unless you subscribe in the meantime. Anyone who brings someone with their invitation code gets 3 free months for each person they bring who subscribes; free months add up.
