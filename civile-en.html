Snags to fix

The client points, you tap the plan.

A crack, a crooked skirting board, a socket to move. Tap the spot, take the photo, give it to a worker. A red dot means right now.

Once fixed it turns green. At handover you walk round with the client and close everything, with a signed PDF to send.

Your margin

You know which job is losing you money.

Enter the quoted hours for each job. ceck compares them with the real ones, including rework and redone jobs.

You see it while you work, not after the job is closed. And on the next quote you know what to charge.

The site survey

Walk the site. The report is already done.

For each thing to fix you take a photo, mark what is wrong with your finger and give it to a worker or a firm: open, in progress, to check.

The plan keeps the point and where you were looking from. The PDF with photos and plan goes out in an email written by ceck.

The measurement survey

Need to write down two measurements on site and nowhere to put them? One finger is enough.

Take a photo of the opening, zoom with two fingers, hold your finger from one end to the other and type the measurement. A magnifier helps you hit the exact point.

On the plan you mark where you stood and which way you were looking. The PDF with photos and measurements goes to the supplier.

The schedule · real screen

See the future of the job. And the future is money.

The schedule comes from your quote: one row per job, with dates. The red line is today, the purple one is when you will really finish. You see problems before they arrive and bring forward what you can: every day saved is money earned.

The client sees the schedule, not your figures. Invite them for free: they see where you are and when you finish. No hours, no prices. Fewer phone calls.
