Painter and decorator: how to organise jobs and final touch-ups

As a painter and decorator, you've just finished a room. The next day another trade comes in and damages a wall. Who does the touch-up? Who pays for it? What state did you leave the room in?

Photograph the finished work

A photo of the walls just finished, with date and room, is proof of how you left the room. If someone then damages it, you can show the damage came afterwards and ask for the touch-up to be paid.

Tasks by room

Preparation, filling, sanding, mist coat and finish coat are different tasks. If you log hours separately by room and by stage, you know how long each one really takes and you see where time is lost.

Materials

Paints, colours, finishes and consumables need to be there when you need them. A single list per job, with the colour code and quantity, saves you stopping halfway through a room or buying the wrong shade.

Small touch-ups

At the final walk-round lots of small imperfections come up. A list with a photo and the exact spot of each one lets you organise the touch-ups without walking round the whole job again.

In short

  • ✅ Photos of the walls when the work is finished
  • ✅ Hours by room and by stage
  • ✅ Materials list with colours and quantities
  • ✅ Touch-ups with photo and location

Finishing a room is one thing. Knowing exactly which rooms are finished is another.
