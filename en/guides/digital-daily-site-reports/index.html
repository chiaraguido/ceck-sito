Digital daily site reports: why keep filling in the same data every evening?

It’s 6 pm.

The crew has gone home.

You’re still on site, or in front of the computer, trying to piece the day together.

Who worked in the bedroom? How many hours did it take? Did the materials arrive? Was that problem sorted?

How much time do you spend on reports?

Say it takes you 20 minutes a day to fill in the report.

With 22 working days a month, that’s 7 hours and 20 minutes.

Almost a full working day every month spent just rebuilding information.

A digital report isn’t necessarily automatic

Turning a paper sheet into a form on your phone is already a step forward.

But if you still have to enter everything a second time, the problem isn’t really solved.

The report should be the result of the work

When you log the workers’ hours, you’re already producing information.

When you update a task, you produce more.

When you record a problem or materials, you document another moment of the day.

Why not use that information to put the report together?
