Site diary example: how to write up a day on site

How do you record a working day on site?

Here is a real example of an internal site record, based on something that could happen during a flat refurbishment.

Example of a working day

Job: flat refurbishment.

On site: four workers.

Hours worked: nine hours each.

Total labour: 36 hours.

Work done:

  • plasterboarding the living room;
  • preparing the bedroom walls;
  • fixing the suspended ceiling grid.

Materials received:

  • 20 plasterboard sheets;
  • 15 metal studs and tracks;
  • two boxes of screws.

Problems found:

  • a socket is not where it should be;
  • some boards are missing to finish the ceiling.

Planned for tomorrow:

  • finish the walls;
  • check the socket position;
  • order the missing materials.

Why does this information matter?

If work stops the next day, you can check straight away whether the problem had already been reported.

If the client asks about the hours, you can look up the work that was recorded.

If you have to plan a similar job, you have useful information to organise it better.
