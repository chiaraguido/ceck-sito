How to manage the construction handover and the final snags

The construction handover is when the client looks at everything carefully, room by room. If you turn up unprepared, they write the snag list, and closing the job drags on for weeks.

An organised handover does three things: you arrive with as few defects as possible, you record properly the ones the client points out, and you can prove when they were put right.

Before handover: your own walk-round

A few days before, do the full walk-round yourself, as if you were the client. Room by room, look at walls, ceilings, floors, doors, windows, sockets, taps and finishes.

Everything that needs fixing is written down straight away, with a photo, the exact spot on the floor plan and who has to fix it. That way the trades know what to do without walking round again.

During handover: the client's snags

These are the defects the client points out at handover. For each one write:

  • where: room and exact spot;
  • what: clear description, with photo;
  • on what: floor, wall or ceiling;
  • who has to fix it;
  • by when.
"Scratch on the door" is not enough. "Bedroom 2, entrance door, 5 cm scratch on the hallway side, 1 m from the floor, joiner, by Friday" gets fixed first time.

Get the client to confirm the list: a signature, an email or a handover record, with the date. It is the basis for closing the job without arguments.

After handover: check and close

When a trade says "done", don't close the snag on their word. Check it, take a photo of the finished work and only then mark it as resolved.

In the end you have a complete list: what the client asked for, who fixed it, when and with which photo. If anyone disputes it, you have the evidence.

The most common mistakes

  • snags written by hand on a sheet that then gets lost;
  • vague descriptions, which mean going back to ask;
  • nobody named as responsible, so nobody does anything;
  • snags closed without checking and without a photo.

In short

  • ✅ Your own walk-round before the client's
  • ✅ Every snag with a photo, a spot on the floor plan, a person responsible and a date
  • ✅ List confirmed by the client
  • ✅ Closed only after checking and a photo of the finished work
