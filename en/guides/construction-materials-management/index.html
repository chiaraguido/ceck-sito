Construction materials management: how to stop your gang waiting for materials

At eight in the morning the gang is ready, but there is no adhesive. Someone has to down tools, take the van and go to the merchant. Construction materials management is about never having that morning.

It seems like nothing. But an hour in the van, plus operatives waiting or half working, quickly becomes a serious cost. Good materials management on site is there so the gang never stops.

Get tomorrow's work ready today

During the day operatives see which materials are running low. The problem starts when they say it out loud, in passing, and then nobody remembers.

A shared list, always open, collects what is needed as it comes up. At the end of the day whoever orders already has everything in front of them, without asking each person.

What to write in every request

  • the material, with the name the supplier uses;
  • the quantity and unit: pieces, bags, metres, litres;
  • the job it has to be delivered to;
  • the date it is needed;
  • the supplier to use.
A request like "need screws" is not enough. "Self-drilling screws 3.5×25, 2 boxes of 1,000, Rome Road job, for Thursday" is ordered in a minute and arrives right.

Fewer phone calls and fewer trips

If every request goes off on its own, you end up making three phone calls and two trips for materials you could have ordered together. By collecting requests in a single list:

  • you place one order per supplier;
  • you organise deliveries around the programme;
  • you avoid duplicates, because you can see what has already been requested;
  • the van stays on the job, not on runs to the merchant.

Consumables: the easiest to forget

Silicone, tape, screws, discs, bags, gloves: they cost little, but if they run out they stop the work just like a thousand-euro material. It pays to have a fixed consumables list for every job, with the quantities usually needed, checked every week.

In short

  • ✅ One list per job, open to the whole gang
  • ✅ Material, quantity, job, date and supplier in every request
  • ✅ Orders grouped by supplier, not one at a time
  • ✅ Consumables checked every week

Tomorrow morning the operatives should be working, not waiting for materials.
