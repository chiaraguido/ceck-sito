WhatsApp for construction: why it isn't enough to run a site

WhatsApp is handy, fast and everyone uses it. For construction, talking to operatives and suppliers on it is often essential. But communicating and running a site are two different things.

Where's that photo?

Three weeks ago someone sent a photo of a socket. Today you need it. Which group was it in? Who sent it? Which room was it?

Scrolling through hundreds of messages to find it is wasted time. And often the photo is there, but with no indication of the exact spot.

Information gets mixed up

A chat ends up with photos, material requests, appointments, problems, voice notes and work instructions all together. The latest message is easy to find. The important one isn't.

  • the gang's hours don't add themselves up;
  • a reported snag has no person responsible and no "resolved" status;
  • whoever joins the group later can't see what was said before;
  • when you change phone, or someone leaves the group, the information is lost.

Organise instead of searching

A site management tool lets you find straight away, without searching:

  • hours worked;
  • the photos of a room;
  • snags still open;
  • material requests;
  • the programme;
  • daily site reports.

You don't need to drop WhatsApp

WhatsApp can stay for talking. ceck is for something else: keeping the job's information in order, linked to rooms, tasks and the people who have to deal with them.
