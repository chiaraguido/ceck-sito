Running a small building firm without spending your evenings in the office

In a small building firm you are tradesperson, buyer, site manager and admin all at once. By day you work. In the evening you have to sort out everything that happened during the day.

You don't need a complicated system

A gang of five operatives doesn't need the same software as a big construction company. It needs simple, fast tools you can use right there on site, on your phone.

The essential information

Every day you should know:

  • who worked and where;
  • how many hours were used;
  • which jobs are finished;
  • what is missing to carry on;
  • which problems need sorting;
  • when the work is due to finish.

Write it down once

If you log hours during the day, you don't have to write them out again in the evening for the daily site report. If you photograph a snag on its spot on the floor plan, you don't have to hunt for the photo again to explain where it is.

If you still use paper today, start by logging hours by task: see construction timesheets.
