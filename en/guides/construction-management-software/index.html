Construction management software: does a small building firm really need it?

You don’t need fifty employees to lose control of a building site.

Four workers, two live jobs, one late delivery and a bit of rework are enough.

The phone keeps ringing, photos pile up on WhatsApp and hours get written down on different sheets.

The problem isn’t the amount of work. It’s information scattered everywhere.

What should good construction management software do?

Software built for a small building firm should help you answer simple questions quickly:

  • Who worked today?
  • How many hours were spent?
  • Which jobs are finished?
  • Which problems need fixing?
  • What materials are needed tomorrow?
  • Are we still on schedule?

It shouldn’t force you to become an IT expert.

Sorting materials before they run out
