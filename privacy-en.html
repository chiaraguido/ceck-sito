Who sees it

Inside a ceck, everyone sees only what their role allows: one company never sees another company’s data. Outside the company: only the publisher of ceck for support (see below) and the technical providers listed below. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Where it is

In the European Union: server in Finland (Hetzner), backups in the EU. Automatic translations go through DeepL (Germany). Card data is handled only by Stripe (Ireland): ceck never sees it.

Security

Encrypted connection (https), encrypted passwords, backup every night, each company in a separate database. If data is stolen or lost, we notify the companies concerned and, where required, the CNIL within 72 hours.

Support access

The publisher of ceck accesses data only to solve a problem, fix a defect or protect the service: access is limited to the data needed, justified and logged. For site data it follows the documented instructions of the company (Art. 28 GDPR). The data stays confidential.
