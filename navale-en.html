Snags

The walkthrough with the owner, on the deck plan.

The captain or the surveyor points at a panel, a joint, a visible screw. Tap the spot, take the photo, give it to your worker. A red dot means right now.

Once fixed you check it and it turns green. On the next walkthrough you open a clean list and close everything, with the PDF to send to the yard.

Extra hours

Extra hours you actually get paid for.

Every job outside the contract has its own sheet: who asked for it, before and after photos, the workers and the hours. The manager signs on the phone.
